== Plant Server V1.1

* main.scad
** added labeled customizer section (poly quality, cut sliders, opening, print gap), mirroring the sensor case style
** added `_top` / `_bottom` toggles (equivalent of the sensor case's `_left` / `_right`) to show/hide each half independently
** added `_model_up` / `_model_down` toggles to show/hide the reference components independently by which half houses them (up: ESP + USB debug pcb, housed in the top; down: relay module, housed in the bottom)
** added `_print` toggle + `view_print()` to lay both halves flat, ready to slice (equivalent of the sensor case's `_print` / `view_print()`)
** renamed `cut_x`/`cut_y`/`cut_z`/`top_open` -> `_cut_x`/`_cut_y`/`_cut_z`/`_top_open` for naming consistency with the sensor case
* `view_print()` orientation (bottom floor-down as modeled, top flipped 180deg so its flat outer face sits on the bed) is a best-guess default, not verified against an actual slice/print -- no OpenSCAD CLI is available in this environment. Please check orientation in a slicer before printing.
** added `display_clip()` + 4 barbs on the display corner posts (`box_top`) so the display pcb clips into place when pushed up instead of just resting loose -- new `_dclip_*` customizer params to retune the fit. Barb height/position is derived from the T-Display S3 pcb geometry (61x25.7x1.2mm) assuming it sits flush against the display border when fully seated (`_top_open=0`); not verified against an actual print/fit, please check and retune `_dclip_*` if it's too tight/loose.
** fix (v2): barb was on the wrong side of the post (reaching in x, when the post sits outside the pcb's y edge, not its x edge) and one pcb-thickness too high -- now reaches in y off the post's y-face, and shifted down by 1.2mm so the ramp/catch sits against the pcb's bottom face instead of its top. Also had to shrink `_dclip_protrusion` (2.0 -> 0.3): the display_hole cut already removes material starting 0.35mm inside the pcb's edge, so there's only a thin window to reach into -- worth double-checking against the real board/print.
** fix (v3): display pads were too short to back up the barb -- their bottom (z=11.3) sat above the barb's ramp start (`_dclip_base_z`=10.4), leaving ~0.9mm of the barb unsupported/floating below the post. Pad bottom is now computed from `_dclip_base_z` (with a small margin) so it always reaches down far enough, and stays in sync if `_dclip_base_z` is retuned.
** fix (v4): the (9,14.5) corner pad+clip collided with the newly-enabled reset button (reset sits around x=6.5-10.5, y=10.85-13.85, z=12.6-14.6 in case coordinates) -- moved that corner to (14,14.5), 5mm further along the same edge, clear of the reset button's footprint. The other 3 corners are unchanged.
** added a pinhole for reset-button access -- same d=1 size convention as the sensor case's boot-button pinhole.
** fix: pinhole was drilled straight down through the top face, but the reset switch is side-actuated (actuator sticks out toward +y at y=12.85, not upward) -- redone as a horizontal hole along y, running from the outer wall (y=33) through the open cavity to the button.
** added a guide box (3x3mm, y=-33 to -12) around the pinhole so a pin has something to follow from the wall to near the button, instead of crossing open cavity blind. The pinhole cuts straight through it. Picks up a small notch near its far end (~1.7x0.5x3mm corner) from the pre-existing display-window cut, since the button sits right at that window's edge -- doesn't break the guide, just thins the wall on one side near the button.
** fix: that guide box actually ended 1.85mm *into* the button's own footprint (y=-13.85..-10.85, from its rst cube + body cube), not just close to it. Shortened to end at y=-14.85, keeping a clear 1mm margin from the button's nearest edge (-13.85). Side effect: the guide's y-range no longer reaches into the display-window cut at all, so the corner-notch issue above no longer applies either.
** extended the guide vertically (z 12.1-15.1 -> 12.1-20) so it's a rib fused to both the wall (y=-33) and the solid ceiling above (shell's own top face is at z=19.3), instead of a cantilever hanging off the wall alone with its far end floating in open cavity -- avoids needing print support under it.
** fix: that overshot the shell's own top face (19.3), poking 0.7mm out of the case -- this is a plain union, not something the outer shell clips, so anything added past its boundary just protrudes. Capped the guide back to z=12.1-19.3, flush with the top face instead of past it.
** fix: reset button was on the wrong side of the pcb along y. Flipped it in `sub_lilygo_t_display_s3.scad` (local y=-pcb[1]/2 -> +pcb[1]/2), which lands at case y=-12.85 once `render_model()`'s rotate([0,0,180]) is applied (not +12.85). Moved the pinhole to the -y side to match, and moved the display-corner-post fix from (9,14.5)->(14,14.5) back to (9,14.5), applying it instead to the corner that now actually collides: (9,-14.5)->(14,-14.5).
** added relay pcb locating pegs on `box_top`'s "relays pads" -- each pad ended flat with just the axial screw hole, now has a d=3mm peg (matching the relay pcb's own mounting-hole size, `holes[2]`) at its tip so it clips/registers the pcb instead of just resting on the flat face.
** added stiffener ribs to the same 4 relay pads (from a real print+assembly: they flex, being 31mm-long 4x4mm cantilevers hanging from the ceiling). Widened each pad to reach the nearest wall (y=+-33, only 8.3mm from holes[1]/2=24.7) over its upper half (z=3.08-18.58, the half nearer the ceiling anchor) -- roughly halves the effective unsupported length, which is what drives cantilever deflection. Ribs stop exactly at the wall (y=33/-33), not past it.
** increased rib height +20% (15.5mm -> ~18.6mm). Top stays anchored at the pad's own top (18.58, fused into the ceiling); the extra reach comes off the bottom instead (z 3.08-18.58 -> ~-0.02-18.58), extending further down the pad toward the pcb.
** added 4x d=3.6mm pump cable holes through box_bottom's -y wall (y=-33), for the relay outputs' cables running out to the pumps. Evenly spaced across the relay board's own width (66.5mm) at x=-24.9,-8.3,8.3,24.9, z=-14 -- the model doesn't capture individual relay terminal positions, so this is a reasonable default spacing, not tied to the real board's terminals. Check against the real board and retune if needed.
** fix: holes were too low. Raised z +15 (-14 -> 1). That needed `hcut` raised too (0.2 -> 0.6) to move the top/bottom split line up, giving box_bottom's own wall enough height to still be solid at the new hole position (new ceiling ~4.25, leaving ~3.25mm margin above the hole). Verified this only trims material off box_top's own outer wall below the new split -- box_top's own ceiling (z=19.3) is fixed regardless of hcut, everything it actually places (display, buttons, usb, reset guide) sits well above the new split, the relay pads/pegs/ribs/screw-hole are separate additions positioned by absolute z (unaffected either way), and the "insert" mating lip in both halves is computed from h/hcut so it tracks the new split line automatically.
** shortened box_bottom's matching peg ("support for replay PCB", the narrower r=1.3 cylinder) to make room -- z=-14->-14.5, h=2.9->1.9, so its top is now flush with its own shelf (z=-13.55) instead of poking up to z=-12.55. Leaves ~0.13mm clearance to the new peg above it. Check fit on a print.
** fix: after the usb-debug module moved +4.7mm (the earlier 7.5mm-gap fix), its outward connector now protrudes ~4.9mm past the case's +x wall.
** fix (v2, reverted the boss above): resized `main_vol`/`main_pos` instead -- `main_vol[0]` 100->104.9, `main_pos[0]` 4->6.45. Growing vol[0] by +4.9 while shifting pos[0] by +2.45 (half) keeps the -x wall anchored at its old position (-46, where the esp's far edge already only has ~0.5mm clearance) and moves only the +x wall out, to 58.9 -- flush with the connector. Widened `box_top`'s "large hole" and `box_bottom`'s "big hole for pcb" cavities by the same +4.9 (90->94.9) so their left edge stays anchored too, instead of encroaching on that same clearance from the cavity side. The mating "insert" lip scales with `main_vol[0]` automatically and stays centered on `main_pos`, so its margin from the wall (~3mm) comes out the same on both sides without a separate fix. No other content needed moving -- everything else (screw holes, pads, buttons, the reset pinhole) uses absolute coordinates independent of `main_vol`/`main_pos`.
** `view_print()`: buttons are now a separate part instead of being carried along inside `box_top`'s print transform.
** fix: the 180-on-x flip I added was wrong -- `box_top_buttons()`'s own local geometry already has the wide base at the bottom (z 15.1-17.7) and the narrow stem on top, so once separated from `box_top`'s rotation it needed no rotation at all, just a lift onto the bed. The flip had put the wide part on top instead. Removed it.
* geometry changed from v1.0: the display corner posts (added retention barbs), the ESP reference model (enabled the reset button), and the usb-debug module's position/mounting (see below) -- box_top/box_bottom volumes and all other holes/pads are unchanged
* fix (from a real print+assembly): the usb-debug module didn't plug fully into the ESP -- measured 7.5mm gap between the two pcbs' facing edges once actually mated. ESP pcb's facing edge is fixed at global x=20.5 (from its own dimensions), so `render_model()`'s usb_debug_pcb() translate moved from `26+7.5`=33.5 to 38.2 (+4.7mm) to put its facing edge at 20.5+7.5=28.0. Also shifted `box_top`'s "pcb-usb pads" and "screw pcb-usb pads" by the same +4.7mm (40.5->45.2, 25.5->30.2) since they were positioned to match the board's old (wrong) mounting-hole locations -- otherwise the screw posts wouldn't line up with the corrected board. Side effect: the module's outward-facing usb connector now lands much closer to the case's fixed USB-hole cutout (was 5mm short of it, now only 0.35mm short) -- an improvement, not something that needed a separate fix.
* fix (from the same print+assembly): the two plastic top buttons pressed too hard against their pcb buttons. `box_top_buttons()`'s base (the large flange, was `[4,6,2.6]`) made 0.5mm thinner (`[4,6,2.1]`), keeping its top face fixed (where it merges with the stem) and raising the pcb-contact face by 0.5mm instead. The stem originally shared that same bottom face -- shortened it by the same 0.5mm (`[2.8,2.8,5]` -> `[2.8,2.8,4.5]`, top fixed) so it doesn't poke out below the base as a new, much smaller contact point. `view_print()`'s btn_lift updated to match (15.1 -> 15.6).
* sub_lilygo_t_display_s3.scad: enabled the `reset button` block (was copy-pasted in from the older, non-S3 t_display module and left commented out). Position (`-pcb[0]/2+12`, bottom long edge) is inherited unadapted from that module -- worth checking against the real T-Display S3 board layout. Note: `box_top` doesn't yet have a matching pinhole/case opening for it, only for the two existing `bt` buttons.
